About this policy

This policy describes Zynqo, the connected gaming experience operated by Zenvora Labs, including its Android app (com.zenvoralabs.zynqo) and these legal pages. Connected app services use Firebase project zynqo-89308.

Some services depend on the installed build, your choices, connectivity and server availability. Offline play, local observations and server-verified rewards are different features; not every local score or action earns account rewards.

Information the app processes

  • Account and profile: Firebase Authentication user ID, email, sign-in provider and verification state; display name, unique username, selected avatar, preferences and account/activity timestamps. Firebase handles password authentication; passwords are not stored in the player-profile document. Google sign-in exchanges provider identity credentials with Firebase.
  • Gameplay and progression: saved boards and runs, levels, moves, scores, question/answer history, personal bests, game usage and recent play. Eligible online sessions may store issued puzzles, command histories, validation results and abuse-prevention records.
  • Connected rewards: global XP/level, virtual coin balance, reward transactions, achievements, mission assignments/progress/claims, daily and weekly chests, cosmetic ownership, purchase receipts and equipment preferences. Cosmetic purchases use virtual coins, not a payment-card checkout.
  • Practice observations: the shared event outbox can synchronize account-linked game/session IDs, event types, timestamps and counters such as merges, distance, matches, words and completions. These observations are not trusted proof of rewards.
  • Device storage: local saves, settings, game-ordering history, question caches, recent-question IDs, trivia session tokens, pending operations and cached account data are stored on the device. Connected caches are scoped to an account; guest/device practice and some preferences are device-wide.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate players, display profiles and progress, resume supported games, reduce repeated questions, synchronize eligible activity, operate missions and inventory, prevent duplicate claims/purchases, validate rewards and protect accounts. Authorized operators may review account, gameplay, transaction and abuse records for support and administration. Administrative actions can produce private audit records.

Where enabled, public leaderboards display usernames and validated ranking information. Do not put sensitive information in a public username.

Advertising and consent

The app integrates Google AdMob through Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising is gated by build configuration, consent availability, SDK readiness and platform policy; integration does not mean ads are active in every build.

When used, Google's advertising services may process device/app identifiers, advertising identifiers where available, IP/network information, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices under Google's policies. UMP presents choices where required and exposes privacy options when applicable. Available ad behavior depends on those choices and provider configuration.

Open Advertising privacy in the app for available consent controls and consent retry. Optional rewarded ads require your action. Supported server-verified ad benefits send a pseudonymous account ID and claim identifier to Google for verification. Development test benefits are separate from production entitlements.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

Usage analytics: the app's analytics preference is off by default and separate from advertising consent. If enabled, Firebase Analytics receives events about active/returning use, gameplay duration/completion, mission observations/claims and ad delivery, failures, completion and revenue. The SDK also processes associated app/device and installation information. The app does not enable Analytics ad-personalization consent through this analytics toggle.

Optional promotional measurement: when usage analytics is enabled, supported sponsored-content views and opens can create account-linked campaign/event receipts and aggregated daily campaign counts. Requests are deduplicated per account, campaign, day and event. Opening an external promotion may also expose ordinary browser/network information to its destination.

Crash diagnostics: reporting is optional, off by default and available only in approved builds. Zynqo's diagnostic wrapper sends error categories/types and stack traces rather than attaching account IDs or exception-message text. Crashlytics may process installation, device and native-crash information. Automatic collection is disabled; opted-in reports are explicitly sent. Turning it off discards pending reports where possible, but cannot recall reports already sent.

Providers and recipients

  • Google/Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions support accounts, storage and server logic; App Check with Play Integrity in release helps assess request authenticity. These services process service/security information such as tokens, app/device signals and network requests. App Check is not a guarantee against abuse.
  • Firebase Remote Config: retrieves supported configuration, including advertising controls.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: optional reminders use a private device registration, notification preferences/categories and delivery records. Enabling reminders requests device permission. In-app updates are also supported. Offline logout may not stop a previously queued generic notification.
  • Open Trivia Database: eligible quiz topics fetch public questions directly and use session tokens. The provider receives network requests, IP information and relevant request parameters. The question fetch does not send your Firebase account ID or email. Questions are attributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • External links: promotional or provider links may open other services, which operate under their own policies.

Provider infrastructure may process data in countries other than yours. Functions use us-central1; this does not establish the location of every Firebase dataset or provider system.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, Google advertising technologies and Open Trivia Database.

Storage, retention and security

Account-linked records generally remain in the account's storage until deletion or an applicable cleanup process. Some records have expiration or eligibility windows; an expired mission, session or claim is not necessarily physically deleted at expiration. The reviewed source does not establish one automatic deletion period for all gameplay, transaction, audit or infrastructure records.

Analytics, diagnostic reports, infrastructure logs and provider backups have separate controls. Their deployed retention settings and the operator's detailed retention schedule have not been verified for these draft pages. No fixed deletion deadline is promised here.

The implementation uses authenticated access, Firestore rules, App Check, server validation and transaction receipts to restrict protected operations. These measures do not guarantee that every threat or loss can be prevented.

Your choices and requests

You may play supported games offline, edit supported profile fields, change settings, control advertising privacy, disable optional analytics/diagnostics and manage notification permission. Connected rewards and purchases require an eligible connected profile. Some optional data already transmitted can remain after a setting is disabled.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other handling of personal information, or complain to a relevant regulator. Use the verified contact channel below once configured; account ownership may need to be verified. No unavailable export or external deletion tool is promised.

See Account Deletion for the implemented in-app process, retained records and the external-request-channel gap. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete the cloud account.

Audience and policy updates

Zynqo is a general-audience gaming app, not a service specifically designed to target children. This statement is not a claim of children's privacy certification. The publisher must confirm the store audience, applicable age requirements and any parental-consent obligations before publication.

This policy may be updated as implemented features or practices change. The effective date identifies the published revision; material changes should be communicated appropriately.
