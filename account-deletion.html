Delete your account in the app

Zynqo includes a Delete account action and a backend deletion function. This website does not call that function or authenticate you. The deployed end-to-end deletion flow still needs the publisher's manual verification.

  1. Open Zynqo and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile and scroll to the account controls.
  3. Choose Delete account and read the permanent-deletion warning.
  4. Confirm using your current password for an email/password account, or reauthenticate with Google for a Google account.
  5. Keep an internet connection while the request is processed. If an error appears, deletion may be incomplete; use the displayed error/retry flow or the verified support channel once available.

The authentication entry also exposes deletion for certain existing-account states, including email verification. Do not share passwords, sign-in tokens, verification codes or recovery codes with support.

Deletion is permanent. Signing out, uninstalling Zynqo or removing these website pages does not delete your cloud account.

What the implemented deletion removes

  • The Firebase Authentication account and active sign-in sessions through account removal/token revocation.
  • The user's Firestore subtree, including profile/preferences, connected gameplay records, reward and transaction records, coin/XP progress, missions, achievements, cosmetic inventory and equipment.
  • Published leaderboard entries linked to the account and its username reservation.
  • Private push registrations/ownership references handled by the deletion cleanup.
  • After a successful in-app deletion response, account-scoped saves and pending references on that installation through the app's local cleanup.

The backend uses a durable deletion job and retry mechanism for partial failures. This is not a promised completion deadline. Offline installations, other devices and independent backups may still contain local copies; cloud deletion cannot remotely erase every local device preference.

What is separate or retained

  • A minimal private marker containing the account UID, deletion status and completion timestamp is retained to prevent late requests from recreating deleted content. No automatic retention limit for this marker is defined in the reviewed source.
  • Device-wide practice data, ad frequency controls, consent/analytics preferences and other non-account-scoped local settings are not all cleared by account deletion.
  • Previously transmitted Analytics/Crashlytics data, provider logs, administrative audit records outside the user subtree and provider backups are not automatically erased by this account-deletion function. Their applicable retention/deletion procedures require separate operator/provider handling.
  • Deleting Zynqo does not delete your Google account or records independently controlled by third-party providers.

The publisher must confirm deployed retention settings, any lawful retention obligations and a practical support process before publication. No unverified deletion duration or blanket erasure guarantee is stated.
